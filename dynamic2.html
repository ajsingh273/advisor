SETUP

TERRA-38 is a spin-tracking and bookkeeping tool, not a prediction system. On an RNG-based electronic table (e.g. Interblock EBT), each spin is statistically independent of the last — the sector score reflects recent history, not future odds. American roulette carries a −5.26% house edge on every bet type here regardless of sector size, and no staking progression changes that expected value; it only reshapes the distribution of outcomes (frequent small results vs. rare large swings). Recovery progressions can require bet sizes that outgrow table maximums during a long losing streak — check your machine's per-number limit before playing.
