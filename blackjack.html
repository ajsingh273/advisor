Strategy Notes

Basic strategy: This tool recommends the mathematically preferred basic-strategy action for the configured rules.

Insurance: This tool does not recommend insurance. Basic strategy generally says not to take insurance without a card-counting advantage. W Wizard of Odds +1

Double: If the chart says DOUBLE but the machine does not permit doubling that hand, HIT instead.

Surrender: Surrender recommendations depend on the number of decks and whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17. W Wizard of Odds

Important: The blackjack payout itself does not normally change the individual hit/stand/double/split chart, but 6:5 blackjack pays materially worse than 3:2. W Wizard of Odds
