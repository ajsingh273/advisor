The Intellectual Heritage of Binary Intelligence

Gerolamo Cardano (1501–1576): Pioneered early statistical analysis of binary chance in Liber de Ludo Aleae, establishing that single trials are erratic while underlying ratios govern outcomes.

Pierre de Fermat & Blaise Pascal (1654): Formulated expected value theory, proving that random choice operates within strict mathematical boundaries.

Jakob Bernoulli (1654–1705): Formulated the Law of Large Numbers in Ars Conjectandi, mapping how empirical frequencies converge over time while exposing short-run variance clusters.

Claude Shannon (1948): Developed Binary Entropy theory, quantifying uncertainty, predictability limits, and information density in chaotic data streams.
